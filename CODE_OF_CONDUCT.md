# Code of Conduct

HeadMates is made for plural systems, including people with DID and OSDD. Many people here are sharing something personal just by showing up. Please help keep this a calm, kind and safe place for everyone.

## Be respectful

- **Treat everyone with kindness**, whether they're plural, singlet, a contributor or just passing by.
- **Use the names and pronouns people give you.** Each alter is their own person: address whoever is talking to you.
- **Don't question anyone's plurality or diagnosis.** No "fakeclaiming", no demanding proof, no debating whether someone's experience is real.
- **Don't ask for private or medical details.** People share what they want, when they want. If you're curious, ask kindly and accept "no".
- **Don't diagnose others.** This is a place for a Minecraft mod, not for medical opinions.
- **Disagree about code, not about people.** Feedback on ideas and pull requests is welcome; insults, mockery and personal attacks are not.

## Not allowed

- Harassment, threats or hate speech of any kind.
- Jokes or content that mock DID, OSDD, plurality or mental health.
- Sharing someone's private information or messages without their permission.
- Sexual content, and any content involving minors in a harmful way.
- Deliberately triggering or upsetting people.

## If something goes wrong

If someone makes you uncomfortable, you can report it privately to the maintainers at **Discord**. Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who don't follow this code. Usually a friendly reminder comes first.

## Take care of yourself

Talking about plurality can be heavy. It's always okay to step away, mute a thread or take a break. If you're struggling, please reach out to someone you trust or to a local support line.
